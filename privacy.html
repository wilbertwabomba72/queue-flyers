Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 29, 2026

Overview

Queue Flyers is designed as an offline single-player puzzle game. The app does not require an account, does not include advertising, does not include in-app purchases, and does not require an internet connection to play.

Data Collection

Queue Flyers does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data, camera data, or device identifiers for tracking.

Local Game Data

The app may store game progress, level completion, settings, and current run state locally on your device. This data is used only to restore gameplay and preferences. It is not sent to us or to a third party by the app.

Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

Queue Flyers does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or cross-app tracking.

Children's Privacy

Queue Flyers does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, no personal information from children is transmitted by the app.

Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored game data by deleting the app from your device. If the app provides an in-game reset option, using that option will reset progress stored by the app on the device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. Any update will be reflected by changing the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions about Queue Flyers, contact the app publisher through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
